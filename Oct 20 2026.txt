### The Bus Day (Oct 19)

### Self and Others
- Pattern: putting himself in others' place (memory of being teased with his ex on a bus), so others' needs always win; friends borrow his scooter and he pays for the auto.
- Money: gets anxious when people are stingy or bargain; he tends to devalue money since college spending; frustrated by discount culture (studio customers haggling).
- Loneliness, FOMO from Instagram, feeling "a different kid" since childhood when others left him out of games; never goes to the park opposite his house.
- Krishna: kindness and self-care can coexist. Give the seat when someone truly needs it; otherwise it's okay to ask others to adjust, share an auto, or keep the scooter.

### The Critical Voice
- A voice says "you're a bad guy, you're putting on a scene to impress someone" (even about waiting for ants to leave his toothbrush). He tends to side with it and accept he's the offender.
- Krishna: treat it like the OCD thoughts. Externalise it as a "cameo appearance", acknowledge it ("I hear you, but I'm not letting you take over") and stand up for himself.
